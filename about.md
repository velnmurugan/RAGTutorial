# About

I'm a Wissenschaftlicher Mitarbeiter (research associate) at the
**Institut für Erziehungswissenschaft, Universität Stuttgart**, in the
department of Berufspädagogik mit Schwerpunkt Technikdidaktik (Technical
& Vocational Education, Technology Didactics). My research sits at the
intersection of AI and technical/vocational education - developing and
evaluating trustworthy AI-supported learning systems that aim to be
meaningful, transparent, and reliable, rather than just convincing.

## Background

I completed my M.Sc. in Mechatronics at Universität Siegen (2021–2025) with 
a focus on robotics, deep learning, computer vision, control engineering,
sensor technology, and embedded systems . I completed my thesis in
collaboration with **Fraunhofer IPA**, contributing to the application
and evaluation of computer vision models for Few-shot object detection.

Along with my Thesis , I was working as a Wissenschaftliche Hilfskraft, 
where I supported in designing a system for analyzing hand movements 
in industrial assembly tasks using pose estimation and 3D mesh models 
to give real-time feedback on manual assembly technique.

I studied Bachelors in Mechanical Engineering (B.E., Anna
University, India, 2016–2020), which is where the dynamic modeling and
simulation background comes from and still shapes how I think about
AI systems in something physically or empirically real, rather
than treating them as black boxes.
