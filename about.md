# About

I'm Vel — an AI and machine learning researcher working on computer
vision, pose estimation, and 3D perception for industrial and scientific
applications. Based in Siegen, Germany, at Universität Siegen.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of deep learning and physical
systems — reconstructing signals from noisy measurements, whether that's
a microscope image, a molecule's geometry, or a boundary condition in a
PDE. I care about models that stay grounded in the physics or the data
they're built on, rather than ones that just look right.

This site is where I write things up: research notes, project
breakdowns, and *Grounded AI* — an interactive, from-scratch tutorial
series on Retrieval-Augmented Generation for people with no ML
background.
